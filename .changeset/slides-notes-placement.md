Importing into Figma Slides now creates real slides — 16:9 pages as the slide's own layers, other sizes scaled to fit — while design files keep the grid of frames that suits comparing several decks. Each new import lands below what is already on the page instead of on top of the last one. PowerPoint speaker notes come along: as an annotation and plugin data on each frame, and in Figma Slides also as a hidden "Speaker notes" layer.
