Missing fonts are replaced by their metric-compatible twin when one exists (Calibri → Carlito, Arial and Helvetica → Arimo, Times New Roman → Tinos, Cambria → Caladea, Georgia → Gelasio, Courier New → Cousine), so text keeps its line breaks; other serif, monospace and condensed fonts fall back to a face of the same kind before Inter.
